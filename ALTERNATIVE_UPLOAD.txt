# ALTERNATIVE UPLOAD METHOD - SIMPLIFIED
# This creates a simple way to upload without needing FTP

## Option 1: Email the file
You can email the file to yourself and upload from email:
1. Attach: jaytibirthday-analytics.js to an email
2. Send to: ekaaiurgaa@gmail.com
3. Download from email on your hosting control panel
4. Upload to your website

## Option 2: Cloud link
Upload to a free cloud service:
1. Upload to: Google Drive, Dropbox, or WeTransfer
2. Get the shareable link
3. Use your hosting control panel to download from link
4. Place in: /assets/js/jaytibirthday-analytics.js

## Option 3: Direct upload script
If you have SSH access, use this command:
curl -X PUT -T jaytibirthday-analytics.js https://jaytibirthday.in/assets/js/jaytibirthday-analytics.js

## EASIEST METHOD:
1. Open your hosting control panel
2. Find "Upload Files" or "File Manager"
3. Upload: jaytibirthday-analytics.js
4. Move to: /assets/js/ folder

## AFTER UPLOAD:
Add to your HTML: <script async defer src="/assets/js/jaytibirthday-analytics.js"></script>
