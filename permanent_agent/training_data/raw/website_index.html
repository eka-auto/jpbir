Dear Jayati,

When I let you in, I didn't do it halfway. I gave you my trust, my quiet fears, my whole belief in us. I built a place in my heart and called it yours — and I have never once pretended those days weren't real.

We were good together. We still could be. That is the thought I keep returning to: what we had was not small, and it was not finished — it was only interrupted. Somewhere along the way, something went wrong. I think, if you are honest with yourself, you feel it too. I'm not writing this to hold it over you. I'm writing because pretending it never happened would cost us far more than gently admitting it ever could.

There were hard days. There were moments I could have walked away, and I didn't — because I kept choosing you, again and again. Loving you was never the mistake, and hoping was never the mistake. I kept the door open longer than most hearts could bear.

So here is the truth, said softly: the door is still open — only now it opens from your side. Open it to say the sorry that was never said. Open it to feel, honestly, the weight of what happened — not to be punished for it, but because owning it is how a person finds their way back. We are both still in a good place in our lives; nothing here is broken beyond repair. And when you have carried that, open the door and come home. I am not closing it. I am only waiting for you to choose it.

With more tenderness than anger, and more hope than either.

— Always

✿

P.S. The light by the door is the same one you left. I never turned it off.
